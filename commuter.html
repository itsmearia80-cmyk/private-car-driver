FAQ

Do I need to reserve in advance?

Yes. Reserve your seat before the departure; bookings close hours before departure. Your seat is subject to confirmation by PRIVARO on WhatsApp.

How many passengers can travel?

Seats on each departure are limited. Availability is confirmed by PRIVARO on WhatsApp before your booking is final.

What happens if the minimum number of passengers is not reached?

If a departure cannot run, PRIVARO will tell you on WhatsApp before departure and agree the options with you.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Message PRIVARO on WhatsApp as early as possible with your booking reference. Cancellation terms are confirmed when your booking is accepted.

Can I purchase multiple trips?

Yes, see Commuter Passes above. Passes are route-specific and arranged via WhatsApp.

How do I confirm my payment?

After PRIVARO confirms your seat on WhatsApp, you receive payment instructions there. A booking is not confirmed or paid just because you sent the WhatsApp message.

Where exactly is the pickup point?

The pickup area is shown on each route. The exact point is shared when your booking is confirmed.
