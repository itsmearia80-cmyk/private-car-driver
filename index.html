Service notes

Can you deliver documents and parcels?

Yes. Our Delivery service carries documents, parcels and goods that fit in the car, from pickup to drop-off. Larger or heavier items are quoted via WhatsApp.

What items can't be delivered?

Illegal goods, drugs and weapons; explosives, fuel and other flammable or hazardous materials; and anything the driver cannot legally carry. Other items may be declined by the driver.

How is delivery priced?

By the estimated distance with a minimum fare, plus a surcharge for larger items. Toll and parking are not included.

Can I book an airport transfer?

Yes. Airport pickup and drop-off are supported.

What is the difference between Book Your Ride and a Package?

Book Your Ride is priced by the estimated distance, one way or round trip, with waiting time charged separately. A Package is a fixed price for 3, 6, 12 or 24 hours with included kilometers.

Can I book a round trip?

Yes.

Is waiting time included?

Waiting time is charged separately.

How is the fare calculated?

For rides, the estimated fare is based on the estimated road distance and selected waiting time. For deliveries, it is based on the estimated road distance (with a minimum fare) and the item size.

Are tolls and parking included?

No. Toll and parking fees are not included in the estimated fare unless specifically agreed.

Is the displayed fare final?

No. The website provides an estimate. Final fare is confirmed by the driver.
